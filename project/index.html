Your Cards, Your Character.

In Daggerheart, every spell and ability your character has is a card. The challenge? You can only bring five into each adventure in your "loadout," while the rest wait in your "vault." This tool was built to make that choice easier. Browse the gallery, build your deck, and manage your reserves quickly and intuitively so you're always ready for your next session.
